## problem

1. HER2-positive breast cancers overexpress the HER2 receptor, a tyrosine‑kinase transmembrane protein that drives uncontrolled cell proliferation, making the disease biologically more aggressive than HER2‑negative tumors.
   - [www.breastcancerspecialist.com.au](https://www.breastcancerspecialist.com.au/surgical-procedures/targeted-cancer-therapies) Dr Chantel Thornton > Breast Cancer Management > Targeted Cancer Therapies
2. Before trastuzumab, HER2 overexpression was linked to shorter disease‑free and overall survival, high recurrence rates, and increased mortality, indicating a poor prognosis.
   - [cir.nii.ac.jp](https://cir.nii.ac.jp/crid/1390001204273749120) Development of HER2-specific humanized antibody Herceptin (trastuzumab). | CiNii Research
   - [www.curetoday.com](https://www.curetoday.com/view/long-term-data-show-the-impact-of-herceptin-on-treating-her2-positive-breast-cancer) Long-Term Data Show The Impact of Herceptin On Treating HER2-Positive Breast Cancer | CURE
3. Trastuzumab (Herceptin) dramatically improved outcomes, reducing mortality in HER2‑positive tumors by about 33 % and bringing survival to levels comparable with HER2‑negative disease.
   - [www.sciencedirect.com](https://www.sciencedirect.com/topics/medicine-and-dentistry/trastuzumab) Trastuzumab - an overview | ScienceDirect Topics
   - [www.breastcancerspecialist.com.au](https://www.breastcancerspecialist.com.au/surgical-procedures/targeted-cancer-therapies) Dr Chantel Thornton > Breast Cancer Management > Targeted Cancer Therapies
4. The original antibody that binds HER2, muMAb 4D5, was generated in mice; fully murine antibodies are recognized as foreign by the human immune system, provoking a Human Anti‑Mouse Antibody (HAMA) response.
   - [www.iedb.org](https://www.iedb.org/details_v3.php?type=assay&id=5566741) Immune Epitope Database (IEDB)

5. The HAMA response leads to rapid clearance of the mouse antibody and can cause allergic or other adverse reactions, preventing effective repeated dosing.

## near_death

1. Genentech initially showed strong reluctance to develop Herceptin, with the company explicitly stating it "wanted no part of it" after the 1987 Science paper linking HER2 to aggressive breast cancer.

2. This reluctance stemmed from Genentech’s recent experience of failed cancer drug projects, making its leadership risk‑averse toward new oncology candidates.
   - [laskerfoundation.org](https://laskerfoundation.org/winners/herceptin-a-targeted-antibody-therapy-for-breast-cancer) Herceptin—a targeted antibody therapy for breast cancer - Lasker Foundation
3. Within Genentech, there was considerable opposition, including skepticism that monoclonal antibodies could work against solid tumors and concerns about the limited market (only ~20‑25% of breast cancer patients).
   - [laskerfoundation.org](https://laskerfoundation.org/winners/herceptin-a-targeted-antibody-therapy-for-breast-cancer) Herceptin—a targeted antibody therapy for breast cancer - Lasker Foundation
4. Despite this internal opposition, a small group of champions—particularly H. Michael Shepard, supported by Vice President Bill Young—persisted in advocating for the HER2 antibody program, keeping the project alive.

## pivotal_trial

1. The trial enrolled 469 women with previously untreated HER2‑positive metastatic breast cancer and randomized them to chemotherapy alone or chemotherapy plus trastuzumab.
   - [sage.cnpereading.com](https://sage.cnpereading.com/doi/10.2217/17455057.5.2.135) Role of Trastuzumab in the Adjuvant Treatment of HER2-Positive Early Breast Cancer
2. Adding trastuzumab to chemotherapy increased median overall survival from 20.3 months to 25.1 months, an improvement of about 5 months.
   - [sage.cnpereading.com](https://sage.cnpereading.com/doi/10.2217/17455057.5.2.135) Role of Trastuzumab in the Adjuvant Treatment of HER2-Positive Early Breast Cancer
3. The same addition improved objective response rate (50% vs 32%) and median duration of response (9.1 vs 6.1 months).
   - [sage.cnpereading.com](https://sage.cnpereading.com/doi/10.2217/17455057.5.2.135) Role of Trastuzumab in the Adjuvant Treatment of HER2-Positive Early Breast Cancer
4. Cardiac dysfunction occurred in 27% of patients receiving trastuzumab with anthracycline/cyclophosphamide, with up to 16% experiencing NYHA class III/IV heart failure.
   - [www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pubmed/11870163) Cardiac dysfunction in the trastuzumab clinical trials experience - PubMed
5. When trastuzumab was combined with paclitaxel, cardiac dysfunction was observed in 13% of patients, and the overall incidence of cardiac events in the trial was 28% for the anthracycline‑trastuzumab arm and 11% for the paclitaxel‑trastuzumab arm.
   - [www.oncologynewscentral.com](https://www.oncologynewscentral.com/drugs/monograph/16623-399019/trastuzumab-intravenous) Trastuzumab: uses, dosing, warnings, adverse events, interactions

## adjuvant

1. The combined interim analysis of NSABP B‑31 and NCCTG N9831 demonstrated that adding one year of trastuzumab to AC→T chemotherapy significantly improved 10‑year overall survival (84% vs 75.2%) and disease‑free survival (73.7% vs 62.2%) with hazard ratios of 0.63 for OS and 0.60 for DFS, indicating a 37% reduction in death risk and a 40% reduction in recurrence risk.
   - [pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4489560) HER2 Targeting in Early Breast Cancer: More Options and More Questions - PMC

2. The HERA trial, which enrolled 5,102 women, showed that one year of trastuzumab after chemotherapy reduced the risk of a disease‑free survival event by 24% (HR 0.76) and the risk of death by 26% (HR 0.74) compared with observation, confirming the durability of benefit over a median 11‑year follow‑up.
   - [pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28215665) 11 years' follow-up of trastuzumab after adjuvant chemotherapy in HER2-positive early breast cancer: final analysis of the HERceptin Adjuvant (HERA) trial.

3. These trial results established one year of adjuvant trastuzumab as the new standard of care for HER2‑positive early‑stage breast cancer, providing large, durable improvements in both DFS and OS.
   - [onco.cc](https://onco.cc/trials/hera-b31-n9831) HERA, NSABP B-31 & NCCTG N9831… · NCT00045032 · OnCo
4. Based on the interim analysis of NSABP B‑31 and NCCTG N9831, the FDA granted its first adjuvant approval for Herceptin on November 16 2006 for HER2‑positive, node‑positive early‑stage breast cancer in combination with AC→TH chemotherapy.
